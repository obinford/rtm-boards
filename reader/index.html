RTM Pick Reader

ESPN doesn't publish picks while a draft is running, so your Mac reads them from the ESPN draft room and your phone shows who to take. Read-only: it never clicks or drafts anything.

One-time setup on your Mac (about 3 minutes)

  1. Download RTM Pick Reader (Chrome extension)
    Double-click the zip in Downloads. Move the rtm-pick-reader folder somewhere it will stay (for example Documents). Don't delete it later: Chrome runs the extension from that folder.
  2. In Chrome, go to chrome://extensions, turn on Developer mode (top right), click Load unpacked, and choose the rtm-pick-reader folder.
  3. Click the puzzle-piece icon in Chrome's toolbar, pin RTM Pick Reader, then click it. Scan its QR code with your phone, open the link, and on your phone tap Share → Add to Home Screen.

On draft day

  1. Mac: plug it in and open your ESPN draft room in Chrome. That's it: the extension starts by itself (a green "RTM" badge appears in the corner) and keeps the screen awake. Keep the draft room as the front tab.
  2. Phone: open the RTM board from your home screen. It shows YOU ARE ON THE CLOCK and who to take; make the pick in the ESPN app.

If the Mac stops sending for any reason, the phone turns grey and says so within about 20 seconds. If ESPN reloads the draft room, the extension picks up again by itself.

Backup: bookmark

Only needed if the extension ever fails. Paired with your extension: this bookmark sends to the same phone link.
RTM Pick Reader (bookmark)
Drag it onto your bookmarks bar (Cmd+Shift+B shows the bar). To use it, click it while the ESPN draft room is open; it opens a small board window. For the bookmark to use the same phone link as the extension, open this page from the extension's "Backup bookmark" button.
