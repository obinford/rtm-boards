How it ranks
Lock-In counts one game per player per week — the one you lock. /wk locked = his expected locked score each week: consensus projection (Sleeper + ESPN + FantasyPros, scored with your league's live settings), his real game-to-game swings (±), how often he plays (ESPN projected games, calibrated on real 2024-25 and 2025-26 results: whole weeks missed + games missed inside weeks), and his team's games each week. ▲ = top-10% ceiling (Lock-In rewards boom games) · ▼ = bottom-10% availability · SPLIT = projection sources disagree by 20%+ (the projection itself is uncertain). ROOKIE = no NBA games yet (not discounted; wider range of outcomes). clear absence = he's back: stop counting a researched injury (stored in this browser; restore undoes it). Recommendations add roster fit, value over replacement, and whether a player survives to your next pick. In a statistical tie, once you already have an injury risk, the board takes the healthier player instead of stacking another. Works in Sleeper mock drafts too (practice mode).
